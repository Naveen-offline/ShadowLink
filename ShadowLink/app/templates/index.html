Cybersecurity awareness

Phishing sites often use valid HTTPS certificates. The padlock means the connection is encrypted, not that the site is honest.
Be wary of urgency ("account suspended"), look-alike domains and links that arrive unexpectedly. Type the official address yourself.
No tool can guarantee a link is safe. ShadowLink gives an estimate with stated limitations, not a verdict.
